## 1.1 Sum insured and premium
Neem Essential (SAMPLE) offers sum insured options of Rs 5,00,000, Rs 10,00,000, Rs 25,00,000. Premium depends on the age of the eldest insured person, the sum insured and the number of persons covered. Premiums are indicative and subject to the insurer's underwriting.

## 2.1 Initial waiting period
No claim is payable for illness in the first 30 days from the policy start date. This does not apply to accidents.

## 2.2 Pre-existing diseases
Claims for pre-existing diseases are payable only after 48 months of continuous coverage.

## 2.3 Specific illness waiting period
Claims for listed conditions such as cataract, hernia and joint replacement are payable only after 24 months of continuous coverage, unless caused by an accident.

## 2.4 Maternity
Maternity expenses are not covered.

## 3.1 Exclusions
The policy does not cover cosmetic surgery, dental treatment unless caused by an accident, and treatment taken outside India.

## 3.2 Room rent and co-payment
Room rent is limited to 1 percent of the sum insured per day. The insured pays 10 percent of every admissible claim.

## 4.1 Cashless claims
For cashless treatment at a network hospital, the insurer must be informed at least 48 hours before planned admission, or within 24 hours of an emergency admission. The plan has 6500 network hospitals.

## 4.2 Reimbursement claims
For treatment at a non-network hospital, claim documents must be submitted within 30 days of discharge.

## 5.1 Restoration and bonus
The sum insured is not restored once it is used up. No-claim bonus: 5 percent per claim-free year, up to 25 percent.

## 5.2 Free-look period
The policyholder may cancel within 15 days of receiving the policy document and receive a refund of the premium, less the cost of any medical examination.
