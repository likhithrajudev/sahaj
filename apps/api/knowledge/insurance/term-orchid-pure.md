## 1.1 Cover and policy term
Orchid Pure Term (SAMPLE) pays the sum assured to the nominee if the life assured dies during the policy term. Sum assured can be chosen from Rs 25,00,000 to Rs 10,00,00,000. Cover can run up to age 75. There is no maturity benefit.

## 2.1 Premium and grace period
Premium depends on age, sum assured and whether the life assured smokes. Premiums are fixed at purchase. A grace period of 30 days applies to unpaid premiums, during which cover continues.

## 3.1 Suicide exclusion
If the life assured dies by suicide within 12 months of the policy start date, the nominee receives only the premiums paid, not the sum assured.

## 3.2 Riders
For this plan, waiver of premium on disability is included; critical illness cover is not available.

## 4.1 Claim process
The nominee must inform the insurer of the death and submit the policy document, the death certificate, the claimant's identity proof and the insurer's claim form. The insurer may ask for further documents if the cause of death needs to be confirmed.

## 5.1 Free-look period
The policyholder may cancel within 15 days of receiving the policy document and receive a refund of the premium, less stamp duty and the cost of any medical examination.
