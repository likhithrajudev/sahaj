## 2.1 Processing fee
A one-time processing fee of 2 percent of the loan amount is charged at disbursal.

## 2.2 Penal charges
A penal charge of 2 percent per month applies on overdue instalments. No other penal charge applies.

The borrower may prepay the loan in part or in full at any time without a prepayment charge.

## 4.1 Cooling-off period
The borrower may exit the loan within 3 days of disbursal by repaying the principal and proportionate interest, without any penalty.

Complaints can be raised with the lender's grievance redressal officer. If unresolved within 30 days, the borrower may approach the RBI Ombudsman.
