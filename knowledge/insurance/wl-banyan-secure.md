## 1.1 Cover
Banyan Secure Whole Life (SAMPLE) pays the sum assured to the nominee on the death of the life assured at any age up to 99, provided premiums are paid. Sum assured can be chosen from Rs 5,00,000 to Rs 50,00,000.

## 1.2 Type of plan
This plan is non-participating: it pays only the guaranteed sum assured and does not take part in profits.

## 2.1 Premium payment
Premiums are payable for 25 years. Cover continues for life after that without further premiums. A grace period of 30 days applies to unpaid premiums.

## 2.2 Surrender
The policy can be surrendered after 3 years of premiums. The surrender value is lower than the premiums paid in the early years.

## 3.1 Exclusions
If the life assured dies by suicide within 12 months of the policy start date, the nominee receives only the premiums paid.

## 4.1 Claim process
The nominee must inform the insurer of the death and submit the policy document, the death certificate, the claimant's identity proof and the insurer's claim form.

## 5.1 Free-look period
The policyholder may cancel within 15 days of receiving the policy document and receive a refund of the premium, less stamp duty and the cost of any medical examination.
